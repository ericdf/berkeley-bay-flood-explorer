Berkeley Bay Flood Explorer

Pick a Bay water level and see which ground, streets and places in west Berkeley sit below it. An educational comparison tool: not a flood forecast and not an official hazard map. Every level names its datum (feet NAVD88) and its source.

Rain on this tide

A rainstorm while the Bay is at the level above, with the storm drain outfalls unable to discharge. An illustrative storage experiment: each watershed's runoff comes up at its lowest inlet and fills the ground around it (model rule). It can overstate water at that inlet and miss ponding elsewhere.

What Berkeley controls. A plan changes the ground, never the water; results compare it with no plan at the same level.
