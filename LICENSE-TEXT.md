## Third-party data keeps the publisher's terms

Data from other sources is not relicensed by this repository. Each dataset keeps its
publisher's terms, recorded in `DATA_INVENTORY.md`. In particular, street data derived
from OpenStreetMap is © OpenStreetMap contributors and available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/), which
requires attribution and that a shared adapted database stay under the ODbL. Works of
